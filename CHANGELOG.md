- Add a music arcade cabinet icon to the application list.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- HINATA-compatible USB NFC reader for Flipper Zero.
- Physical FeliCa and supported MIFARE Classic cards.
- Saved Lite-S files with present/remove controls.
- On-device card picker and diagnostics.
- Official and Unleashed firmware builds.
- English and Simplified Chinese documentation and contribution guides.
